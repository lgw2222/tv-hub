Harmony Hub

Harmony sends the TV's own infrared power code, which works even when the TV is fully off. Add each TV as a device in the Harmony app first, and turn on Enable XMPP in the app (Settings, Harmony Setup, Add/Edit Devices & Activities, Remote & Hub). The hub needs to be able to "see" the TVs, or use Harmony IR mini blasters for TVs in other spots.

Turning TVs on when they're fully off

A TV that's fully off drops off Wi-Fi, so nothing can reach it until it wakes. The hub sends a wake-up signal to the TV's MAC address, but the TV has to be set to listen:
Roku TVs: Settings, System, Power, Fast TV start: On.
Google TVs: Settings, System, Power & energy, turn on network standby / Remote start / Wake on LAN (name varies by brand).
Fire TV built into the TV: Settings, Display & Sounds, Power Controls, turn on wake / network standby options.
Fire Sticks: plug the stick's power into a wall outlet, not the TV's USB port, and turn on HDMI-CEC on the TV so the stick can switch the TV on.

Only needed on newer Google TVs. On the TV: Settings, System, Developer options, Wireless debugging, Pair device with pairing code. Enter the port shown after the colon and the six-digit code. Then set that TV's ADB port to the connect port shown on the Wireless debugging screen.
